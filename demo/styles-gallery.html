Heart

Definition

A muscular pump in the circulatory system, located in the center of the thorax, enclosed by the pericardium, driving blood flow throughout the body day and night.

Daten

7,500 L

Daily blood output; approximately 100,000 beats, weighing 250 to 350 g.

Struktur

4

Chambers: left atrium, left ventricle, right atrium, right ventricle; four valve sets ensure unidirectional flow.

📌 What you will learn

The heart is the muscular pump of the cardiovascular system, about the size of a fist. We will explore its four chambers, four sets of valves, and how it pumps roughly 7,500 liters of blood per day.
